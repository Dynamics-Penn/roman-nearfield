Here we provide the key data released together with the Koposov, Erkal et al (2023)
paper:

The contents of dataset

* best_fit_oc_model.fits -- the best fit model of the OC stream presented in
the paper in the oblate potential
* measurements -- folder with various spline measurements of stream track/density and possible stream members
* models -- Stan models
* potential_samples -- MCMC posterior samples from fitting of the OC
stream in different potentials
* mass_profiles -- the measured mass profiles of LMC and MW as shown on Figures 15 and 17
