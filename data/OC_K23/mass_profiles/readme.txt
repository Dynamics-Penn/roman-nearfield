The tables contain the constraints of the mass profiles for the MW (fig 15)
( oblate_mass_table.fits ) 
and LMC (fig 17)
( lmc_mass_table.fits)

Each table has the following columns

r_arr: distance in kpc
M_arr: median mass within distance r_arr from the posterior in units of 10^10 Msun
dM_p_arr: +1sigma uncertainty on the mass (in units of 10^10 Msun)
dM_2p_arr: +2sigma uncertainty
dM_m_arr: -1sigma uncertainty
dM_2m_arr: -2sigma uncertainty
